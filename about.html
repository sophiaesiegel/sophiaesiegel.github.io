About me

Background, research, and writing.

I use GIS to explore, analyze, and visualize environmental patterns in a changing climate — quantifying hazards, conservation and marine spatial planning, monitoring change over time, and site suitability for energy and resources.

Previously with the Micheli Lab at Hopkins Marine Station, the Sperling Historical Geobiology lab, and the Center for Ocean Solutions' Indonesia blue foods project — and an EPIC fellow at the Palau International Coral Reef Center in Koror, working on marine spatial planning for the Palau National Marine Sanctuary.

I also write for environmental and climate publications — most recently on the West Coast seaweed industry for & the West (Bill Lane Center for the American West), along with Arena Magazine and The Pacific — and document travel and research through photography.
