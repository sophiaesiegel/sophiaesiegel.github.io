Shore Check

Shore Check is a prototype early warning system for harmful algal blooms. It puts California's official shellfish advisories from the California Department of Public Health next to NOAA's C-HARM forecasts of domoic acid risk, so you can see where an advisory is already in force and where the forecast says one may be coming. It is a prototype, not a safety tool: always check with CDPH before harvesting shellfish.
